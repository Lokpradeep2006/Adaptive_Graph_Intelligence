ABSTRACT | 3 | Modern smart-city infrastructures rely on large-scale Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystems consisting of sensors, cameras, gateways, controllers, and critical monitoring devices. These environments continuously generate massive volumes of heterogeneous security telemetry, making it difficult for traditional security monitoring systems to identify coordinated and evolving cyber threats. | The proposed project, " | IoT Threat Twin for Smart-City Defense: Multi-Agent Correlation, Drift-Aware Behavior, and Autonomous Response | ", introduces a next-generation intelligent security framework that transforms raw IoT telemetry into contextual attack narratives and actionable defense intelligence. | IoT Threat Twin creates a living security representation of the environment capable of understanding what happened, explaining why it happened, predicting what may happen next, and recommending optimal response actions.

--- ppt/slides/slide4.xml ---
ABSTRACT | 4 | The framework integrates six major intelligence layers: | IoT Telemetry & Adaptive Deception Layer | Captures real-world attacker behavior using IoT devices, smart-city infrastructure logs, and adaptive IoT deception mechanisms. | Drift-Aware Behavioral Analytics Layer | Continuously learns device, user, and service behavior while distinguishing legitimate operational changes from malicious deviations. | Temporal Knowledge Graph Engine | Correlates security events across time to reconstruct multi-stage attack chains and infer hidden attack relationships. | Network Provenance Graph Engine | Models device-to-device interactions and attack propagation paths to detect lateral movement across smart-city infrastructure. | Multi-Agent Threat Intelligence Layer | Employs collaborative AI agents including Log Analysis Agents, Threat Intelligence Agents, Correlation Agents, Response Agents, and Report Generation Agents for autonomous investigation and decision support. | Autonomous Response & Threat Narrative Layer | Generates explainable attack narratives, predicts potential attack progression, recommends mitigation strategies, and assists security analysts in rapid incident response.
